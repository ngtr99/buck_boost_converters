Project Overview

This project presents two essential DC-DC converter topologies: buck and boost converters. These circuits are widely used to step voltage down or up with high efficiency and stable output performance.

Buck Converter

A buck converter steps down input voltage to a lower regulated output using a switch, inductor, diode (or synchronous rectifier), and capacitor.

  • High efficiency: Lower power dissipation than linear regulation.
  • Stable regulation: Reliable output under varying input/load conditions.
  • Low-voltage rails: Commonly converts 12V/24V to 5V or 3.3V.
  • Compact thermal profile: Reduced heat and cooling requirements.
  • Industry usage: Consumer electronics, automotive, and embedded systems.

Boost Converter

A boost converter steps up input voltage to a higher output level, making it ideal when source voltage is below load requirements.

  • Voltage step-up: Raises low battery or source voltage efficiently.
  • Battery systems: Maintains useful output as battery voltage declines.
  • Higher-voltage loads: Supports LEDs, displays, motors, and communication modules.
  • Energy applications: Common in solar and energy-harvesting designs.
  • Flexible integration: Fits mixed-voltage architectures.
